Enjoy support

Enjoy saves links, beer, and wine in one place, with a share-sheet button on iPhone and a browser extension.

Getting help

  • Report a problem or ask a question: open an issue at https://github.com/pajamaw/enjoy-site/issues. Please don't include passwords or invite codes.
  • Your account or data on a shared server: contact the person who gave you your invite code; they run that server.

Common questions

I can't sign in. Check the invite code or server address. A shared server may be offline for a while; things you save are kept on your phone and sync when it is back.

How do I delete my account? In the app: Settings > Delete account. This removes your account and everything in it from the server and from the phone.

A saved page has no title or tags. The server could not fetch it yet. Save the same page again to retry.

How do I undo a delete? Tap Undo in the bar that appears after deleting.
