Terms of Use — Enjoy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Enjoy is an app for keeping a private list of things you save and rate. These terms cover the app and the shared Enjoy server run by the developer, Peter Wickwire. If you use a server that someone else runs, that person sets the rules for it; these are the minimum.

Your list is private

What you save is visible only to you, unless you choose to share an item with a group.

Sharing with a group

When you share an item with a group, every member of that group can see it, including your rating and review of it. Only share things you would be happy for everyone in the group to see.

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour. You must not share anything that is:

  • abusive, harassing, threatening or hateful;
  • sexually explicit or exploitative;
  • illegal, or that promotes illegal activity;
  • spam, or a link meant to deceive or harm.

Reporting and blocking

  • Report: every item someone else shared has a Report option. A reported item is hidden from you straight away and the report goes to the person who runs the server.
  • Block: you can block any member of a group you are in. You will no longer see what they share, and they will not see what you share.
  • A group's owner can remove members.

On the developer's server, reports are reviewed within 24 hours. Content that breaks these terms is removed from the group, and the person who shared it can be removed from groups or have their account closed.

Your account

You can delete your account and all its data at any time in Settings → Delete account. The person who runs a server may close accounts that break these terms.

No warranty

Enjoy is provided as it is, without warranty. Keep your own copy of anything you cannot afford to lose.

Contact

Questions and reports that cannot be made in the app: see the support page.
